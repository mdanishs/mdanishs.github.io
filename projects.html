Product · Next.js, Node.js

HireDoc

An interview-based resume tool for software engineers who moved to Canada or the US. Instead of paraphrasing your existing resume, it interviews you to surface the impact you left out, then rebuilds your experience around it. Built and shipped solo, end to end — interview engine, generation, payments, and the free diagnostic quiz.
