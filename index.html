Why good engineers get ignored here

“Back home, I got interviews in weeks. In Canada, I’ve been applying for six months without a single callback.” That is the most common message in my inbox, and almost every time, the problem isn’t a lack of skill.

It’s a lack of representation. The North American market reads resumes differently than services markets do. Back home, a resume is a record of responsibilities and tenure. Here, recruiters screen for business impact — scale, ownership, architecture decisions, and measurable outcomes.

A great engineer with a poorly positioned resume loses to an average engineer who communicates value clearly. Most people respond by applying more. That’s not how this market works.
